Frogging Nook

Nepal's crochet community — designed, built, and grown from zero.

Client Frogging Nook
Year 2026
Industry Crochet Community · Events
Scope Brand Identity · Tech · Marketing
froggingnook.com
The Brief

Frogging Nook is Nepal's crochet community — hands-on workshops, meetups and crochet-alongs across Kathmandu, Lalitpur and Bhaktapur. A community, not a product. Our brief was the whole thing: design and build its digital home — accounts, a live events system, tutorials — and grow the community itself from a standing start.

" Connection is more powerful than competition — the nook's line, and the brief behind everything we made.
iisiichaa.com
The Partner

It began with Iisiichaa.

The idea for a crochet community came from Iisiichaa. We partnered with them to make it real — shaping the brand, building the platform, and growing the audience — and together we ran the workshops, meetups and crochet-alongs that turned the idea into a community that meets in person.

One Partner, End To End

Three domains, one team.

Frogging Nook needed a brand, a platform, and an audience — so we did all three. One team across identity, engineering and marketing, each one feeding the next.

01 — Brand Identity

An identity built on connection.

Before the product, the brand. We built Frogging Nook's whole identity — the mark, a full logo system, a custom icon set, the type and the palette — around one belief: connection is more powerful than competition. Stitch. Share. Belong.

The Mark

Each rounded petal stands for a maker, a learner, a creator — intentionally separate, but connected. Together they bloom into the Nook: a shared space for creativity, connection and new beginnings.

The wordmark carries the rest — a bold-yet-gentle type that reflects strength, warmth, and the rhythm of creating, undoing, and growing together.

02 — Tech

A digital home for the nook.

We designed the experience and engineered it end to end — a Turborepo of Astro + React apps on a Node server, plus a dedicated admin panel for the team, all containerized with Docker.

01

Join the Nook

Accounts done properly — sign-up with email verification and member profiles that tailor event recommendations to each person's experience level.

02

A living events engine

Workshops, meetups and crochet-alongs in one system, filterable by type — with a 'Next up' card that flips to a live 'happening now' state and nav cues that light up when an event is on the schedule.

03

Its own learning app

Free, structured tutorials live in a dedicated app of their own — so anyone can pick up a hook and start, no sign-up required.

04

Gallery, live from Instagram

Moments from real sessions pulled straight from the feed, so the site and the social work stay one and the same.

05

A back office for the team

A dedicated admin panel — built with TanStack Start and React — lets the team publish events, tutorials and stories through a built-in markdown editor, no developer needed.

06

Built to scale

A Turborepo of Astro + React apps on a Node server, with a Prisma-backed data layer, all containerized with Docker — fast, secure, and easy to run.
