Case Study · Brand Identity

Ghumgham

Travel, made local.

Client Ghumgham.com
Year 2026
Industry Travel & Tourism
Scope Brand Identity · Design
Brand Story

Ghumgham.com is Nepal's all-in-one travel platform, built on over a decade of local expertise to make travel more accessible, reliable, and culturally relevant. Not simply a booking tool but a travel companion — built around the realities, emotions, and needs of travellers at home and abroad, and made to replace uncertainty with confidence.

" A dependable travel companion — present before, during, and after every journey.
The Mark

A pin, a peak, a promise.

The G is drawn as a location pin and a compass ring, with a Himalayan skyline resting in its negative space — Nepal held inside the mark itself. One quiet symbol for place, direction, and the journey between them.

Outcome

What we shipped.

01

Nepal, held in the mark

The monogram folds a location pin, a compass ring and a Himalayan skyline into a single G — one symbol for place, direction, and the journey between them.

02

A palette with altitude

A confident orange pulled from Nepali warmth, grounded on deep espresso. Bold enough for a billboard, warm enough to read like a local welcome.

03

One system, every touchpoint

From the app icon to a taxi topper to a letterhead, the mountain wave and monogram hold together — so Ghumgham reads as itself everywhere it shows up.
